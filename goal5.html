Tristan Recipe for the Best Kangkong Chips

Ingredients

For The Chips :

  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 1/4 teaspoons yeast
  • 3/4 sugar
  • 1/4 salt

For The Filling :

  • 2/3 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 coffe
  • 3/4 sugar
  • butter

Instructions :

  1. Mix the warm milk, yeast, sugar, egg, and egg yolk in a large bowl.
  2. Melt the butter and add it to the mixture.
  3. Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  4. Once the dough has doubled in size, roll it out into a large rectangle.
  5. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon together, then sprinkle the mixture evenly over the butter.
  6. Bake in a preheated oven until golden brown and cooked through.
