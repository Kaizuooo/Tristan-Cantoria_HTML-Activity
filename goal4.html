Spider Man

Peter Parker is known for his intelligence, courage, and strong sense of responsibility. After the death of his Uncle Ben, Peter learns the important lesson that "with great power comes great responsibility." He uses his abilities to protect innocent people while trying to balance his life as a student, friend, and superhero. Spider-Man has become one of Marvel's most popular and recognizable characters, appearing in comic books, movies, television shows, and video games
